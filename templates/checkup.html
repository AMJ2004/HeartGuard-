Regular Checkups

Regular screening is key to early detection and heart health management

High blood pressure and high cholesterol can damage your heart and blood vessels. But without testing, you probably won't know whether you have these conditions. Regular screening can tell you what your numbers are and whether you need to take action.

1. Blood Pressure

Regular blood pressure screenings usually start in childhood. Starting at 18, your blood pressure should be measured at least once every two years. If risk factors present, screen annually.

2. Cholesterol Levels

Adults should have cholesterol measured every 4-6 years starting at age 20. Earlier if family history of heart disease.

3. Type 2 Diabetes Screening

Diabetes is a major heart disease risk factor. Screening recommended starting at age 45, or earlier if overweight or family history.

✅ Recommended Action
Schedule regular checkups to monitor weight, blood pressure, cholesterol, and blood sugar. Talk to your doctor about personalized screening.
